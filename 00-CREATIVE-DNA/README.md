# Phase 0 — Creative DNA

**Status:** IN PROGRESS  
**Purpose:** Define the emotional and artistic DNA of the manga before locking title, world, power system, characters, or Chapter 1.

## Working Method

This document is the persistent Phase 0 checklist. It is updated as decisions are made.

- **CANON** = explicitly approved direction; safe to build on.
- **PROVISIONAL** = interesting idea under discussion; not canon yet.
- **REJECTED** = explicitly ruled out; do not reintroduce unless the creator changes the decision.
- **OPEN** = still needs to be explored.

Conversation brainstorming does **not** automatically become canon. A decision becomes canon only after Gopal approves it.

## CANON — Current Decisions

### 1. Feeling Over Features

The manga should not be built by collecting popular ingredients or forcing every possible feature into the story. Characters, places, relationships, mysteries and conflicts should naturally create emotion.

The goal is for readers to genuinely feel what the story is asking them to feel: love, excitement, sadness, happiness, curiosity, fear, wonder, laughter, shock, attachment and emotions we have not yet imagined.

The story should earn its emotions. No tragedy merely for shock, no twist merely because twists are exciting, and no spectacle merely because it looks cool.

### 2. Adventure Is Unpredictable

Adventure can produce discovery, wonder, comedy, mystery, attachment, danger, beauty, horror, heartbreak, or something completely unexpected. The location, characters and circumstances determine the experience rather than a fixed formula.

Different places may create completely different kinds of stories. A peaceful place, ridiculous situation, terrifying location, beautiful civilization, tiny village or seemingly insignificant encounter can all become meaningful.

### 3. World Beyond the Protagonist

The world must ultimately feel larger than Gopal. This principle is already established in MASTER-PLAN.md and remains part of the Creative DNA: the world should continue living beyond Gopal's immediate story.
