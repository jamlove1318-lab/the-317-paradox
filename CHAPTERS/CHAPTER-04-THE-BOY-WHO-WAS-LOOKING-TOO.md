# Chapter 4 — The Boy Who Was Looking Too

**Status:** PROVISIONAL

## Page 1
The alley behind Orrin's east market is narrow enough that Gopal has to turn sideways to avoid a hanging basket.

The mysterious boy is already there.

**Boy:** "You're weird."

Gopal blinks.

**Gopal:** "That's a fast introduction."

The boy points at the navigation object.

**Boy:** "You carry a compass that points at things that don't exist."

Gopal looks at it.

**Gopal:** "It's not technically a compass."

**Boy:** "That makes it worse."

---

## Page 2

The boy finally introduces himself.

**Boy:** "Kael."

**Gopal:** "Gopal. Nice to meet you."

**Kael:** "Why?"

**Gopal:** "Because now I know what to call you."

Kael stares.

**Kael:** "That's the worst answer I've heard today."

Gopal smiles.

For some reason, he likes him immediately.

---

## Page 3

Orrin has apparently noticed Gopal.

A public notice reads:

> **STRANGER WITH STRANGE COMPASS SEEN NEAR EAST GATE.**

Under it:

> **PLEASE DO NOT GIVE HIM DIRECTIONS.**

And underneath that, in different handwriting:

> **ESPECIALLY IF HE ASKS NICELY.**

Gopal reads it twice.

**Gopal:** "People here are very welcoming."

Kael: "They're worried."

**Gopal:** "About me?"

**Kael:** "About whatever you find."

---

## Page 4

They walk.

Gopal asks the obvious question.

**Gopal:** "Are you looking for the old road?"

Kael doesn't answer immediately.

Then:

**Kael:** "Yes."

**Gopal:** "Why?"

**Kael:** "Because I need to find something."

**Gopal:** "What?"

Kael looks at him.

**Kael:** "If I knew, I wouldn't need to find it."

Gopal pauses.

**Gopal:** "...I think we're going to get along."

---

## Page 5

Kael pulls an old folded map from inside his coat.

It shows Orrin.

The eastern fields.

The old road.

Then a large section has been deliberately torn away.

Gopal studies the edge.

**Gopal:** "Someone ripped this."

**Kael:** "My father."

Silence.

**Gopal:** "Why?"

**Kael:** "He said some maps are safer when they're incomplete."

---

## Page 6

Kael explains.

His father had searched for the old road.

He left Orrin years ago.

He never returned.

There is no dramatic music.

No heroic speech.

Just a boy folding an old map carefully.

**Kael:** "When I was younger, I wanted to find him."

Gopal listens.

**Kael:** "Now I want to know why he went."

Gopal looks at the map again.

That answer matters to him.

---

## Page 7

Gopal doesn't say, *We'll find your father.*

He almost does.

Instead:

**Gopal:** "I don't know what we'll find."

Kael looks surprised.

**Gopal:** "But if you're going there, I'd like to see it too."

Kael folds the map.

**Kael:** "That's a terrible reason."

**Gopal:** "Probably."

**Kael:** "You're still coming?"

**Gopal:** "Obviously."

---

## Page 8

Morning.

At the inn.

Gopal bursts through the door.

**Gopal:** "WE'RE GOING!"

Sera throws a pillow at him.

**Sera:** "WHO IS WE?"

**Bran:** "WHY ARE YOU AWAKE?"

Mel, still half asleep:

**Mel:** "He discovered another problem."

Gopal points toward the window.

**Gopal:** "Kael knows where the old road begins."

Sera sits upright.

**Sera:** "That is not the same as knowing where it goes."

**Gopal:** "Exactly."

Everyone stares.

**Gopal:** "That's why it's interesting."

---

## Page 9

Kael joins them outside.

Bran looks at him.

Then at Gopal.

Then back at Kael.

**Bran:** "Who is this?"

**Gopal:** "Kael."

**Bran:** "Why is he coming?"

**Gopal:** "Because he knows something."

**Kael:** "I know where the road used to begin."

Bran sighs.

**Bran:** "That is significantly less useful."

**Mel:** "Still more useful than what we know."

Bran considers this.

**Bran:** "I dislike how reasonable that was."

---

## Page 10

They return to Edda.

She hears the plan.

Her expression changes before anyone finishes explaining.

**Edda:** "No."

Kael steps forward.

**Kael:** "I'm going."

**Edda:** "Your father said that."

Kael stops.

The room becomes quiet.

Gopal looks at Edda.

**Gopal:** "What happened?"

Edda doesn't answer immediately.

Then:

**Edda:** "He found the beginning."

She looks toward the eastern window.

**Edda:** "He never found the end."

---

## Page 11

Edda gives them a location.

An abandoned stone arch beyond the eastern fields.

No road.

No buildings.

No reason for an arch to be standing there.

**Sera:** "How old?"

**Edda:** "Older than Orrin's current streets."

**Bran:** "That's not a number."

**Edda:** "It's the number you're getting."

Gopal tries not to laugh.

Fails.

Edda looks at him.

He stops.

---

## Page 12

Before they leave, Edda calls Gopal aside.

**Edda:** "If the road appears, don't assume it wants you to follow."

Gopal nods.

**Gopal:** "Okay."

This time, he means it.

Edda studies him.

**Edda:** "You really don't know when to stop, do you?"

Gopal thinks.

**Gopal:** "I'm learning."

**Edda:** "Learn quickly."

---

## Page 13

The group crosses the eastern fields.

Sera carries the map.

Bran carries food.

Mel carries books.

Kael carries the torn map.

Gopal carries too much.

Bran looks at his pack.

**Bran:** "What exactly is in there?"

**Gopal:** "Useful things."

**Bran:** "Such as?"

Gopal opens it.

A spoon.

A broken button.

Three stones.

A rope.

Two notebooks.

A wooden cup.

A bent piece of metal.

A completely unnecessary frying pan.

Bran closes the bag.

**Bran:** "I don't want to know."

---

## Page 14

The stone arch appears.

It stands alone in the field.

No wall.

No road.

Just two weathered pillars and a heavy stone crossing the top.

Kael stops.

His face changes.

**Kael:** "That's it."

Sera looks at the arch.

Then at the map.

Then at the arch again.

**Sera:** "There is no road."

**Kael:** "There used to be."

Gopal walks closer.

The navigation object is silent.

For once.

---

## Page 15

Kael touches the stone.

Nothing happens.

He pulls out his father's map.

The symbol is drawn there.

The same symbol.

Gopal's expression becomes serious.

He takes out his notebook.

**Gopal:** "Same as the marker."

Sera notices.

**Sera:** "And the object."

Mel opens her book.

**Mel:** "And this."

Bran looks at all three.

**Bran:** "Wonderful."

He points at the arch.

**Bran:** "Does anyone have a symbol that means 'normal'?"

Nobody answers.

---

## Page 16

Mel places her palm against the stone.

**Mel:** "Warm."

Gopal touches it.

Cold.

He looks at Kael.

Kael touches it.

Warm.

They look at each other.

Gopal doesn't joke.

**Gopal:** "Did it always feel like that?"

Kael shakes his head.

**Kael:** "I don't know."

The navigation object moves.

Slowly.

It points through the empty arch.

---

## Page 17

There is nothing there.

Just grass.

Sky.

Wind.

Gopal takes a step.

Sera grabs his shoulder.

**Sera:** "No."

Gopal stops.

He remembers Edda.

*Don't assume it wants you to follow.*

A leaf blows toward the arch.

It passes between the stones.

And disappears.

Everyone freezes.

Bran whispers:

**Bran:** "I vote we go home."

---

## Page 18

Another leaf crosses.

Gone.

The air inside the arch bends.

Not like heat.

Not like wind.

Like something invisible has become slightly out of alignment.

Kael steps forward.

Gopal catches his sleeve.

**Gopal:** "Wait."

Kael looks back.

**Gopal:** "We don't know what happens if you go through."

**Kael:** "My father did."

**Gopal:** "That's exactly why we should think first."

Kael stares at him.

Then slowly nods.

---

## Page 19

The world inside the arch changes.

The grass bends.

The air clears.

A road appears.

It stretches through a forest that should not be visible from this field.

The trees are unfamiliar.

The sky has a different color.

Far away, something enormous moves between the trunks.

No one speaks.

Sera's hand tightens around the map.

Mel closes her book.

Bran quietly puts down the food.

Kael whispers:

**Kael:** "He went through there."

Gopal looks at the road.

His expression is not excitement.

Not fear.

Something between them.

**Gopal:** "Then let's find out what he found."

---

## Page 20

The five stand before the impossible road.

Gopal takes one step.

Then stops.

He looks back at Orrin.

The town is still there.

Small.

Crooked.

Ridiculous.

Alive.

He smiles.

Then turns toward the road.

**Gopal:** "We come back."

Sera looks at him.

**Sera:** "You'd better."

The group crosses the threshold.

Behind them, the arch remains.

Ahead—

the road disappears into another world.

### Final Caption

**The first road Gopal chose led away from home.**

**The next one may lead somewhere the world forgot.**

---

### Chapter Purpose / Continuity Notes

- Kael is now established as a recurring companion candidate, not yet a permanent crew member.
- His father remains unresolved. His disappearance is not yet confirmed as death, capture, or survival.
- The old road is established as physically impossible but still unexplained.
- The navigation object, symbol, Mel's book, and Kael's father's map are now visibly connected without explaining why.
- The road appearing through the arch is the first major threshold event.
- Gopal demonstrates a small but important growth point: he remembers Edda's warning and stops Kael rather than acting immediately.
- Orrin remains a living place that can continue existing independently of the protagonists.
- No ultimate mythology, final antagonist, chosen-one explanation, or 317 meaning is revealed.
- The next chapter should pay off the threshold crossing through discovery and character interaction rather than immediately explaining the mystery.
