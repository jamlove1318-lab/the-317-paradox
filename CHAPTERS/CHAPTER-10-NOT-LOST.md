# Chapter 10 — Not Lost

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — STORY DRAFT
**Saga:** 01 — The First Horizon

## 24-page manga script

### Page 01 — A Quiet Morning
Gopal carries water at the boarding house without being asked. The cook notices. “You're learning.” Gopal tells her not to sound surprised.

### Page 02 — Sera's Maps
Sera sits surrounded by the official map, her living map, local notes, bridge records and route names. Gopal tries to understand how they fit together.

### Page 03 — Reality Is Inconvenient
Gopal asks why maps keep changing. Sera says things keep changing. He calls this inconvenient. She agrees that reality usually is.

### Page 04 — Bad Art
Sera lays out Gopal's sketches. One riverbank resembles a sleeping animal; a bridge has six legs; a tree looks like a person. Gopal calls them artistic choices. Sera calls them inaccurate choices.

### Page 05 — But One Is Right
Sera points out that one tiny side path is accurately drawn because Gopal noticed carts using it. He is surprised that his way of observing can actually help.

### Page 06 — Don't Get Proud
Gopal celebrates. Sera warns him not to become proud. He says it is already too late.

### Page 07 — City Life
Outside, Aster Reach continues: market opening, schoolchildren, boats, Civic Guards, musicians and bakers. Nothing pauses for the main characters.

### Page 08 — Bran
Bran passes the boarding house and greets Sera as “Map Woman,” Gopal as “Boy.” They have become familiar enough for the joke to feel established.

### Page 09 — Parcel
Bran needs a parcel delivered north. Sera says the road is closed. Bran asks how she knows. Gopal proudly points at Sera.

### Page 10 — The River Alternative
Gopal asks why they don't use the river. Bran and Sera look at him. He realizes that travel logistics are more complicated than they appear.

### Page 11 — Connected Systems
Bran explains that roads, ferries, rivers and markets affect each other. A closed bridge changes deliveries, which changes prices, which affects families. Gopal sees the first real system behind ordinary life.

### Page 12 — A Road Matters
Gopal says one road can matter to people he has never met. Sera says most things do.

### Page 13 — Home Again
Gopal thinks about Veyra, the road from home, the river, the boat and Aster Reach. His world has expanded without erasing where he came from.

### Page 14 — Sera's Offer
Sera says she is going north tomorrow and asks whether Gopal wants to come. He immediately says yes. She adds that he must follow instructions.

### Page 15 — Conditions
Water. Proper shoes. Equipment. Gopal agrees to everything, then tries to argue that his current shoes are proper. Sera disagrees.

### Page 16 — Bran's Observation
Bran watches them and says they are going to be trouble. Gopal asks if that means together. Bran says definitely.

### Page 17 — Notice Wall
Gopal passes a public notice wall: lost dog, bridge repair, apprenticeship, festival preparation, missing boat hook, market opening, survey work and a small bounty notice.

### Page 18 — Bounty
Gopal asks whether people really get paid to find wanted people. A passerby says sometimes, because Civic Guards can't be everywhere. Gopal studies the notice without romanticizing the job.

### Page 19 — Many Lives
A bounty hunter walks by looking tired. A guard watches him. A merchant complains. A child chases a dog. A musician plays. The city remains ordinary and complicated.

### Page 20 — Ready
Sera rolls up her maps. Gopal says he is ready. She points out that he doesn't know where they are going. He says that's why he is ready.

### Page 21 — North Road
They leave Aster Reach with equipment. Bran tells them not to break anything. Gopal promises. Sera says he probably will.

### Page 22 — Looking Back
At the edge of the settlement Gopal turns around. Aster Reach looks different from outside: not merely buildings, but a place full of lives and routines. He says he thinks he is starting to know it.

### Page 23 — Left
At a fork Sera checks the map and says left. Gopal starts walking, then stops and realizes he actually knows which direction left is. Sera laughs.

### Page 24 — The First Ten Chapters
Full sequence of small images: Gopal and Sera walking north; Bran's boat leaving; the boarding-house cook hanging laundry; Mira receiving Gopal's message; Rajan repairing a cart; Nemi reading it and complaining that Gopal still hasn't answered whether he is eating enough.

Final wide image: the road stretches ahead while Aster Reach remains behind them and the river runs beside the settlement.

**CAPTION:** “Gopal had left home because he wanted to see the world. Ten chapters later, he had learned something he hadn't expected.”

**CAPTION:** “The world was not one great answer. It was thousands of ordinary lives, roads that changed, people who disagreed, places that became familiar, and questions that could wait.”

**FINAL LINE:**

**He wasn't lost anymore.**

**He was going somewhere.**

# CONTINUITY LEDGER — CHAPTERS 3–10

## Gopal
- Curiosity remains his defining strength and weakness.
- Learns that not every mystery is sinister.
- Begins asking before interfering.
- Learns that travel requires money, work and patience.
- Starts belonging to Aster Reach without surrendering his desire to explore.
- Begins seeing connections between ordinary systems and people's lives.

## Sera
- Introduced through actual cartographic work.
- Has an independent goal and expertise.
- Initially sees Gopal as a complication.
- Begins trusting his observations.
- Has invited him on another survey.
- Friendship is only beginning; no instant emotional shortcut.

## Bran
- Recurring river trader with his own livelihood.
- Blunt, practical, money-conscious and quietly generous.
- Recognizes Gopal and Sera as recurring trouble.
- Does not join them.

## Aster Reach
Now established as a living major location with river trade, markets, boarding houses, food, schools, workshops, music, families, Civic Guards, travelers, multiple peoples, local infrastructure, local names and early bounty notices.

## Regional conflict
Only seeds exist: certain routes are avoided; information is uneven; some roads cause trouble. No Rask reveal.

## Supernatural layer
No Relic explanation. No Spiritual Power. No hidden-power reveal.

## Story direction
Chapters 11 onward should continue deepening Aster Reach and the northern survey rather than immediately replacing the region. Future material can naturally introduce festivals, apprenticeships, games, recurring locals, small criminals, bounty hunters, local politics and eventually early supernatural incidents before the Gatekeeper's influence becomes overt.

**Core rule:** the story must continue to feel like Gopal's life unfolding, not a chain of unrelated adventures.
