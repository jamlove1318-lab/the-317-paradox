# Chapter 2 — The Road That Wasn't on the Map

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — opening chapter draft  
**Series:** Original Manga Universe — Gopal  
**Continuity:** Follows Chapter 1, "The Thing That Shouldn't Have Moved"

## Opening Page

A wide view of Veyra.

Morning.

The river is calm.

Smoke rises from cooking fires.

Someone is arguing with a donkey.

A woman is hanging laundry between two buildings.

Children run through the street.

A merchant is already complaining about the price of something.

Nothing has changed.

Except one thing.

Gopal is gone.

---

## Page 2

Mira stands outside the family home.

She watches the road.

Rajan comes beside her.

For several seconds neither speaks.

**RAJAN:**  
"He forgot his spoon."

Mira looks at him.

**MIRA:**  
"He took three."

Rajan pauses.

**RAJAN:**  
"...Then he's ready."

Mira almost smiles.

Almost.

---

## Page 3

Far down the road—

Gopal walks.

His backpack is much too large.

His steps are enthusiastic.

His face is serious.

He is carrying water, food, clothes, his notebook, a repair tool, several completely useless objects, and the mysterious navigation object.

He stops.

Looks back.

Veyra is already small.

Gopal raises one hand.

No one can see him anymore.

He lowers it.

Then—

**GOPAL:**  
"Okay."

He turns around.

Walks five steps.

Stops.

Turns back again.

**GOPAL:**  
"...Definitely not going back."

He walks forward.

---

## Page 4

Three hours later.

Gopal is sitting beside the road.

Eating.

Sweating.

His backpack lies beside him.

He looks at it.

Then at the road.

Then at the backpack.

**GOPAL:**  
"I packed too much."

Beat.

He opens the bag.

**GOPAL:**  
"No."

He looks at the food.

**GOPAL:**  
"I packed exactly enough."

He eats another piece.

---

## Page 5

A wagon approaches.

Two travelers sit in front.

An old woman drives.

A young man beside her sleeps with his mouth open.

Gopal stands.

**GOPAL:**  
"Excuse me!"

The wagon slows.

**OLD WOMAN:**  
"Yes?"

**GOPAL:**  
"Does this road lead to Ressa?"

The woman stares at him.

**OLD WOMAN:**  
"No."

Gopal looks at the road.

Looks at her.

**GOPAL:**  
"Oh."

The woman waits.

**GOPAL:**  
"Where does it lead?"

She points.

**OLD WOMAN:**  
"North."

Gopal looks north.

**GOPAL:**  
"And what's there?"

The woman shrugs.

**OLD WOMAN:**  
"North."

The wagon continues.

Gopal watches it disappear.

He writes in his notebook:

> **Lesson 1: Some people apparently consider directions complete answers.**

---

## Page 6

The road forks.

One path is wide and heavily traveled.

The other is narrow.

Almost hidden by grass.

Gopal checks the navigation object.

The needle—or whatever passes for a needle—points toward the narrow road.

He frowns.

**GOPAL:**  
"That doesn't make sense."

He looks at the wide road.

Then the narrow one.

Then the object.

**GOPAL:**  
"So obviously..."

He takes the narrow road.

---

## Page 7

The narrow road becomes smaller.

The grass becomes taller.

Trees begin appearing.

Gopal walks more slowly.

He looks around.

No houses.

No carts.

No people.

Just forest.

He checks the object again.

The strange needle trembles.

Gopal smiles.

**GOPAL:**  
"You know something."

The needle stops.

Gopal stops.

They stare at each other.

**GOPAL:**  
"...I know you don't have eyes."

The needle moves again.

Gopal's smile disappears.

---

## Page 8

He turns the object upside down.

The needle points somewhere else.

He turns it sideways.

It points back.

He shakes it.

Nothing.

He taps it.

Nothing.

He hits it against his palm.

The needle spins wildly.

**GOPAL:**  
"Stop doing that."

It stops.

Gopal freezes.

A long silence.

He slowly raises his eyebrows.

**GOPAL:**  
"...Did you understand me?"

A bird flies away.

Gopal immediately points at it.

**GOPAL:**  
"You saw that too."

The bird does not answer.

---

## Page 9

Gopal continues.

The forest opens.

He finds something unexpected.

A stone marker.

Half buried.

Covered in moss.

There is a symbol carved into it.

Gopal kneels.

Touches it.

The same symbol appears on the navigation object.

His expression changes.

Not fear.

Recognition.

He removes his notebook.

---

## Page 10

He sketches the symbol.

Underneath it he writes:

> **FOUND AGAIN.**

Then:

> **Second occurrence.**

Then:

> **Location: unknown road.**

He pauses.

Adds:

> **Question: Why does the same mark appear in different places?**

He closes the notebook.

Then notices something.

There are footprints around the stone.

Fresh ones.

Several people.

Gopal stands.

Someone has been here recently.

---

## Page 11

He follows the footprints.

They lead deeper into the forest.

Then—

Voices.

Gopal crouches.

Ahead is a small clearing.

Three people are standing around a broken cart.

One is shouting.

**MAN:**  
"I told you the road was wrong!"

**WOMAN:**  
"You said you knew the road!"

**MAN:**  
"I knew the road yesterday!"

**THIRD PERSON:**  
"That is not how roads work."

Gopal blinks.

The three turn.

They see him.

Silence.

**MAN:**  
"Who are you?"

Gopal stands.

Points at himself.

**GOPAL:**  
"Me?"

**MAN:**  
"Yes."

**GOPAL:**  
"Gopal."

Beat.

**GOPAL:**  
"I'm lost."

The three stare.

**WOMAN:**  
"We're lost too."

Gopal smiles.

**GOPAL:**  
"Oh."

He walks into the clearing.

**GOPAL:**  
"Then we're friends."

---

## Page 12

The three travelers introduce themselves.

**SERA** — a traveling cartographer.

**BRAN** — a trader who insists he understands roads.

**MEL** — a quiet young person carrying far too many books.

They explain that their map says the road should lead to a settlement.

But it doesn't.

The road simply...

ends.

Gopal looks behind them.

The forest continues.

**GOPAL:**  
"Where's the settlement?"

Sera points ahead.

**SERA:**  
"There."

Gopal sees nothing.

**GOPAL:**  
"I don't see it."

**SERA:**  
"Exactly."

---

## Page 13

Sera unfolds the map.

The drawing is detailed.

Rivers.

Hills.

Forests.

Roads.

Settlements.

A red line marks their route.

Gopal compares it with the landscape.

**GOPAL:**  
"This river isn't here."

Sera looks up.

**SERA:**  
"What?"

Gopal points.

A narrow river cuts through the trees.

Sera immediately stands.

She checks the map.

Then the river.

Then the map.

**SERA:**  
"That river doesn't exist."

Gopal smiles.

**GOPAL:**  
"It does now."

Sera does not laugh.

---

## Page 14

They walk to the river.

It is shallow.

Clear.

Beautiful.

Fish move beneath the surface.

Sera crouches.

Touches the water.

**SERA:**  
"This should be three days west."

Bran looks offended.

**BRAN:**  
"Maybe the map is wrong."

Sera slowly looks at him.

**SERA:**  
"My map is twenty years old."

Mel speaks for the first time.

**MEL:**  
"Then maybe the river isn't."

Everyone looks at Mel.

Mel shrugs.

**MEL:**  
"I'm only saying."

Gopal looks at the river.

Something bothers him.

The current is flowing uphill.

He blinks.

Looks again.

Water is moving toward higher ground.

**GOPAL:**  
"...That's not right."

---

## Page 15

Gopal kneels.

The river appears normal.

Except—

A leaf floats downstream.

Then suddenly stops.

It reverses.

Moves upstream.

Then stops again.

Then floats normally.

Gopal's eyes widen.

The same strange feeling from Veyra.

The world doing something it shouldn't.

He reaches toward the water.

Sera grabs his wrist.

**SERA:**  
"Don't."

Gopal looks at her.

**GOPAL:**  
"Why?"

Sera doesn't answer immediately.

**SERA:**  
"Because we don't know what it is."

Gopal looks at the river.

Then at her hand.

She releases him.

---

## Page 16

Gopal sits.

For once, he doesn't touch it.

He watches.

Minutes pass.

Nothing happens.

Bran becomes impatient.

**BRAN:**  
"Wonderful. A river."

He walks away.

Then—

A sound.

Not loud.

Not threatening.

Almost like someone whispering underwater.

Gopal turns.

The others hear it too.

Mel drops one of the books.

The book lands open.

A page contains a drawing of the same symbol.

Gopal sees it.

Freezes.

**GOPAL:**  
"Where did you get that?"

Mel looks at the page.

Then at Gopal.

**MEL:**  
"I was going to ask you the same thing."

---

## Page 17

Silence.

Gopal takes out the navigation object.

Mel takes one step closer.

Sera notices.

**SERA:**  
"What is that?"

Gopal doesn't answer.

The object begins to vibrate.

Very slightly.

The river becomes completely still.

No current.

No insects.

No birds.

Nothing.

Then—

A single bubble rises from the river.

Pops.

And every sound returns at once.

---

## Page 18

Bran falls backward.

**BRAN:**  
"Nope."

He gets up.

**BRAN:**  
"Absolutely not."

He grabs his bag.

**BRAN:**  
"I have decided I hate this forest."

Sera is staring at the water.

Mel is staring at Gopal.

Gopal is staring at the navigation object.

The strange needle points directly toward the river.

---

## Page 19

Gopal looks at the water.

Then at the symbol in Mel's book.

Then at the stone marker.

Three unrelated things.

Same symbol.

He whispers:

**GOPAL:**  
"So..."

He smiles.

Not because he understands.

Because he doesn't.

**GOPAL:**  
"...what are you?"

---

## Page 20

Night.

The four travelers make camp.

A small fire burns.

Bran is asleep.

Sera studies the map.

Mel reads.

Gopal sits awake.

He writes in his notebook:

> **Day 1.**
>
> **I thought leaving Veyra would make the world feel bigger.**
>
> **It does.**
>
> **But that's not the strange part.**
>
> **The strange part is that something feels familiar.**
>
> **I don't know why.**

He stops writing.

Looks toward the darkness.

Something moves between the trees.

Gopal stands.

**GOPAL:**  
"Hello?"

Nothing.

He takes one step forward.

A second sound.

A soft metallic click.

Gopal reaches for the navigation object.

The needle is no longer pointing toward the river.

It is pointing toward him.

---

## Final Page

Far above the forest—

On a distant ridge—

A tiny light appears.

Then another.

Then another.

Three lights.

They move in a straight line through the darkness.

Gopal cannot see them.

The reader can.

Final narration:

> **Somewhere beyond the road, something had already noticed the boy who had just left home.**

**END OF CHAPTER 2**
