# Chapter 05 — The River Changes

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — STORY DRAFT
**Saga:** 01 — The First Horizon

## 24-page manga script

### Page 01 — The Wrong Way
Gopal compares his terrible river sketch with the real river. He insists the river should turn left. The crewman calmly explains that it is turning left; Gopal has simply drawn the bend badly.

### Page 02 — Current
The channel narrows. Crew members work the poles and rudder. Gopal watches rather than grabbing anything.

### Page 03 — Yesterday
A passenger says this wasn't yesterday's route. The crewman replies, “Yesterday isn't today.” Gopal asks how a river can change.

### Page 04 — Seasonal River
The crew points out new sandbanks, submerged trees and a fallen trunk. Water, weather and land constantly alter the route.

### Page 05 — Maps
Gopal asks whether maps can be wrong. The answer is more complicated: sometimes the map is wrong; sometimes it was right when drawn and the world changed afterward.

### Page 06 — The Sentence
The crewman says: “Maps remember yesterday. The river is already tomorrow.” Gopal writes it down.

### Page 07 — Gopal's Map
Gopal proudly reveals his revised sketch. It contains a mountain that is actually a riverbank. The crewman refuses to call it accurate.

### Page 08 — People Who Know
A local fisherman directs the boat through a deeper channel. Gopal learns that some knowledge lives in people's experience rather than documents.

### Page 09 — Many Names
Two fishermen use different names for the same stretch of water. Gopal asks which name is correct. The crewman says both, because different communities remember places differently.

### Page 10 — Rain
A storm arrives. Gopal tries to help cover cargo and gets trapped beneath a sheet. The crewman repeats the running joke: “You're helping. Somewhere else.”

### Page 11 — Weather
Passengers shelter together. Gopal watches rain turn the river silver. The chapter slows down.

### Page 12 — Wait
Gopal wants to go outside. The old woman catches his collar and reminds him of the boat rule. Gopal insists he wasn't going to jump; she tells him she knows what he was thinking.

### Page 13 — Rainbow
The storm passes. A rainbow appears over the river. Gopal simply watches.

### Page 14 — Leaving Room
Gopal crosses out part of his map and writes: “Leave room for being wrong.”

### Page 15 — Towers
Distant structures appear. More boats join the channel. The crew identifies them as Aster Reach.

### Page 16 — Bigger Than Expected
Aster Reach unfolds around the river: warehouses, docks, bridges, markets, workshops and homes. Gopal asks if all of it is one place.

### Page 17 — Different Peoples
Gopal sees several original peoples living and working together: humans, a tall horned riverland people carrying timber, and small winged fruit sellers. No exposition dump; they simply belong there.

### Page 18 — Don't Stare
The old woman catches Gopal staring. A winged vendor jokes with him about never having seen wings attached to someone selling pears. Gopal laughs.

### Page 19 — A Living Place
Gopal sees a school, workshops, musicians, Civic Guards helping civilians, couriers, children and animals. The city feels occupied rather than staged for his arrival.

### Page 20 — It Doesn't Notice Him
The crowd ignores Gopal. Arguments, laughter, work and errands continue. For a moment Gopal is quiet because the world is plainly bigger than his arrival.

### Page 21 — Already Here
Gopal asks how long Aster Reach has existed. The crewman says, “Longer than you.” Gopal admits he likes it; the crewman warns him about the prices.

### Page 22 — Docking
The boat approaches its berth. Gopal helps with a rope correctly. The crewman says, “Better.”

### Page 23 — Solid Ground
Gopal steps onto the dock. Several roads leave the waterfront. He turns back and sees how small the boat suddenly looks.

### Page 24 — The First Horizon
Full spread: Aster Reach, river traffic, markets, bridges, roads, different peoples, smoke, animals, music and distant hills. Caption: “He had spent years wondering what was beyond Veyra. He had expected the answer to be one place. It wasn't. It was a world.”

## Continuity
Aster Reach becomes the main setting. Gopal does not immediately leave. The river journey has taught him that maps, memory and reality can differ. No Rask reveal, no major power reveal, no Spiritual Power.
