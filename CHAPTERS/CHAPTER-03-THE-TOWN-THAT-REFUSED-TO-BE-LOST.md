# CHAPTER 3 — THE TOWN THAT REFUSED TO BE LOST

**Status:** PROVISIONAL  
**Saga:** Opening Journey  
**Chapter:** 3

## PAGE 1 — MORNING

The forest is quiet.

Gopal is asleep beside the cold remains of the campfire.

His notebook is open beside him.

His navigation object lies on the ground.

It is pointing directly at his face.

### PANEL 1
Close-up of Gopal sleeping peacefully.

**GOPAL (sleeping):**  
“Mmm... five more minutes...”

### PANEL 2
The navigation object trembles.

**TIK.**

### PANEL 3
Gopal's nose twitches.

### PANEL 4
His eyes open.

He stares directly at the object.

**GOPAL:**  
“...Were you watching me?”

### PANEL 5
The object remains perfectly still.

**GOPAL:**  
“That's creepy.”

## PAGE 2 — THE CAMP

Sera is already awake.

She is staring at the map.

Bran is attempting to cook something in a dented pot.

Mel sits beneath a tree reading.

### PANEL 1
**GOPAL:**  
“Good morning!”

Nobody answers.

### PANEL 2
**GOPAL:**  
“Good morning?”

Still nothing.

### PANEL 3
**GOPAL:**  
“GOOD MORNING!”

Bran jumps.

**BRAN:**  
“WHY ARE YOU SHOUTING?!”

**GOPAL:**  
“I was checking.”

**BRAN:**  
“Checking what?”

**GOPAL:**  
“If everyone was alive.”

Beat.

**BRAN:**  
“...We're three feet away from you.”

## PAGE 3 — BREAKFAST

Bran proudly serves breakfast.

It is an unidentifiable black mass.

### PANEL 1
**GOPAL:**  
“What is it?”

**BRAN:**  
“Breakfast.”

**GOPAL:**  
“I know. I mean... what was it?”

Sera covers her mouth.

Mel continues reading.

### PANEL 2
Bran looks offended.

**BRAN:**  
“It was bread.”

### PANEL 3
Gopal pokes it.

**GOPAL:**  
“Was?”

### PANEL 4
Mel finally looks up.

**MEL:**  
“Past tense seems appropriate.”

Bran points at her.

**BRAN:**  
“You don't get breakfast anymore.”

**MEL:**  
“I wasn't eating it anyway.”

## PAGE 4 — THE MAP

Sera spreads the map over a flat stone.

### PANEL 1
The map shows their intended route.

A road.

A settlement.

A river.

But the river they crossed yesterday isn't marked.

### PANEL 2
Sera circles the blank area.

**SERA:**  
“This is wrong.”

### PANEL 3
Gopal crouches beside her.

**GOPAL:**  
“Maybe the map is old.”

**SERA:**  
“It's two years old.”

**GOPAL:**  
“That's old.”

Sera looks at him.

**SERA:**  
“It's not.”

### PANEL 4
Gopal points toward the forest.

**GOPAL:**  
“Then maybe the forest moved.”

### PANEL 5
Sera stares.

**SERA:**  
“Forests don't move.”

Gopal considers this.

**GOPAL:**  
“Probably.”

## PAGE 5 — THE OBJECT

Gopal takes out the navigation object.

It points away from their mapped road.

Sera notices.

### PANEL 1
**SERA:**  
“You're following that thing?”

**GOPAL:**  
“It's been right twice.”

**SERA:**  
“Twice?”

**GOPAL:**  
“That's enough to become interesting.”

### PANEL 2
Sera grabs his wrist.

**SERA:**  
“No.”

### PANEL 3
Gopal blinks.

**GOPAL:**  
“No?”

**SERA:**  
“No.”

**GOPAL:**  
“Why?”

**SERA:**  
“Because yesterday it led us to a river that flows uphill.”

Beat.

**SERA:**  
“I'm beginning to distrust your definition of interesting.”

### PANEL 4
Gopal smiles.

**GOPAL:**  
“Fair.”

## PAGE 6 — THE DECISION

They follow the marked road instead.

The forest gradually thins.

The strange river disappears behind them.

The navigation object remains pointed in the opposite direction.

Gopal keeps looking back at it.

### PANEL 1
Mel walks beside him.

**MEL:**  
“You don't have to follow it.”

**GOPAL:**  
“I know.”

### PANEL 2
**MEL:**  
“Then why do you keep looking?”

Gopal thinks.

### PANEL 3
**GOPAL:**  
“Because I want to know why it wants to go there.”

Mel studies him.

**MEL:**  
“That's different from wanting to go there.”

Gopal smiles.

**GOPAL:**  
“Is it?”

Mel returns to her book.

**MEL:**  
“Yes.”

## PAGE 7 — THE TOWN

They emerge from the forest.

A small town sits ahead.

A wooden sign reads:

**WELCOME TO ORRIN**

Below it, someone has scratched:

**WE ARE NOT LOST.**

Then underneath:

**STOP ASKING.**

### PANEL 1
Gopal reads it.

### PANEL 2
**GOPAL:**  
“That's reassuring.”

### PANEL 3
Bran looks at the sign.

**BRAN:**  
“Why would anyone write that?”

### PANEL 4
Sera sighs.

**SERA:**  
“Because travelers keep asking whether this town is lost.”

### PANEL 5
Gopal looks at her.

**GOPAL:**  
“Is it?”

Sera closes her eyes.

**SERA:**  
“Don't.”

## PAGE 8 — ORRIN

Orrin is strange.

Not impossibly strange.

Just... inconvenient.

Every street seems to curve slightly.

Buildings lean in different directions.

The central square has three fountains.

All three point toward different streets.

### PANEL 1
Gopal turns around.

**GOPAL:**  
“Which way is the center?”

A local points behind him.

**LOCAL:**  
“You're standing in it.”

### PANEL 2
Gopal looks down.

**GOPAL:**  
“Oh.”

### PANEL 3
Bran whispers to Sera.

**BRAN:**  
“I hate this town.”

### PANEL 4
Sera whispers back.

**SERA:**  
“We've been here for thirty seconds.”

**BRAN:**  
“Exactly.”

## PAGE 9 — A SMALL REQUEST

A woman carrying a basket bumps into Gopal.

Apples spill everywhere.

### PANEL 1
**GOPAL:**  
“Sorry!”

He immediately starts collecting them.

### PANEL 2
The woman notices the navigation object hanging from his belt.

Her expression changes.

Only for a moment.

### PANEL 3
**WOMAN:**  
“Where did you get that?”

Gopal stops.

**GOPAL:**  
“This?”

### PANEL 4
He holds it up.

**GOPAL:**  
“It belonged to someone else.”

The woman looks away.

**WOMAN:**  
“Then give it back.”

### PANEL 5
Gopal frowns.

**GOPAL:**  
“I was planning to.”

**WOMAN:**  
“To whom?”

Gopal doesn't have an answer.

## PAGE 10 — THE NAME

The woman takes one apple from Gopal.

She examines it.

**WOMAN:**  
“Do you know what it's called?”

**GOPAL:**  
“The object?”

She nods.

**GOPAL:**  
“No.”

### PANEL 2
**WOMAN:**  
“Then don't call it yours.”

### PANEL 3
She walks away.

Gopal watches her.

### PANEL 4
Sera approaches.

**SERA:**  
“You know her?”

**GOPAL:**  
“No.”

**SERA:**  
“She knows that thing.”

**GOPAL:**  
“Apparently.”

### PANEL 5
Gopal looks at the object.

For the first time, he doesn't smile.

## PAGE 11 — THE NOTICE BOARD

Orrin's central square contains a huge public notice board.

It is covered with papers.

**LOST CAT**

**LOST CART**

**LOST BROTHER**

**FOUND BUTTON**

**WHOEVER OWNS A BLUE UMBRELLA PLEASE STOP LEAVING IT ON MY ROOF**

And one handwritten notice:

> **IF YOU KNOW WHERE THE OLD ROAD GOES, DON'T TELL THE MAYOR.**

Gopal reads it twice.

### PANEL 2
**GOPAL:**  
“What's the old road?”

Sera immediately grabs his shoulder.

**SERA:**  
“No.”

### PANEL 3
**GOPAL:**  
“You don't even know what I'm going to say.”

**SERA:**  
“I know you.”

### PANEL 4
Gopal looks at the notice again.

His eyes sparkle.

**GOPAL:**  
“I think I'm going to like this town.”

## PAGE 12 — THE MAYOR

The mayor's office is a small building.

The mayor is an elderly woman named **Edda**.

She sits behind an enormous desk.

She looks at the four travelers.

Then at Gopal.

Then at the navigation object.

### PANEL 1
Silence.

### PANEL 2
**EDDA:**  
“Where did you get that?”

Gopal looks around.

**GOPAL:**  
“Everyone keeps asking me that.”

### PANEL 3
Edda sighs.

**EDDA:**  
“Because you are carrying something that should not still exist.”

### PANEL 4
Gopal leans forward.

**GOPAL:**  
“What is it?”

Edda doesn't answer.

### PANEL 5
Instead:

**EDDA:**  
“Do you know where you're going?”

Gopal smiles.

**GOPAL:**  
“No.”

Edda studies him.

Then, unexpectedly, laughs.

## PAGE 13 — THE OLD ROAD

Edda explains.

There was once an old road leaving Orrin.

Nobody remembers exactly where it went.

The road disappeared decades ago.

Not destroyed.

Not blocked.

It simply stopped appearing on maps.

People who followed old descriptions found forests, hills, and empty land.

Eventually everyone stopped looking.

### PANEL 1
Sera listens carefully.

### PANEL 2
**SERA:**  
“So there really was a road?”

**EDDA:**  
“Yes.”

### PANEL 3
**GOPAL:**  
“Where did it go?”

Edda looks toward the window.

**EDDA:**  
“That is the question.”

## PAGE 14 — THE WARNING

Edda places an old piece of paper on the desk.

A hand-drawn road.

A symbol.

The same symbol.

### PANEL 1
Gopal's expression changes.

### PANEL 2
**GOPAL:**  
“That's it.”

### PANEL 3
Sera looks between the paper and Gopal's object.

**SERA:**  
“The same mark.”

### PANEL 4
Edda nods.

**EDDA:**  
“Your object belonged to someone who searched for that road.”

### PANEL 5
Gopal whispers:

**GOPAL:**  
“Did they find it?”

Edda pauses.

**EDDA:**  
“They never came back.”

## PAGE 15 — SILENCE

Nobody jokes.

Nobody moves.

### PANEL 1
Gopal looks at the object.

### PANEL 2
His fingers tighten around it.

### PANEL 3
Sera watches him.

**SERA:**  
“We don't have to follow this.”

### PANEL 4
Gopal looks at her.

He isn't excited.

He isn't smiling.

**GOPAL:**  
“I know.”

### PANEL 5
**SERA:**  
“Then?”

Gopal looks toward the town outside.

**GOPAL:**  
“I want to find out what happened to them.”

## PAGE 16 — THE ANSWER

Edda shakes her head.

**EDDA:**  
“You're young.”

**GOPAL:**  
“Yes.”

**EDDA:**  
“You don't understand what you're asking.”

**GOPAL:**  
“Probably not.”

### PANEL 2
Edda almost smiles.

**EDDA:**  
“That's an honest answer.”

### PANEL 3
She folds the old paper.

**EDDA:**  
“The road isn't your problem.”

### PANEL 4
Gopal looks at her.

**GOPAL:**  
“Then whose is it?”

Edda answers:

**EDDA:**  
“That's why nobody can find it.”

## PAGE 17 — NIGHT

The town sleeps.

Gopal sits outside the inn.

His notebook rests on his knees.

He writes:

**DAY 2**

**Orrin.**

**There was an old road.**

**Someone searched for it.**

**They disappeared.**

He pauses.

Then:

**The object may belong to them.**

### PANEL 2
He adds:

**Question: Who were they?**

### PANEL 3
Then:

**Question: Why does everyone recognize the mark?**

### PANEL 4
He hesitates.

Then writes:

**Question: Why does the object keep choosing roads?**

## PAGE 18 — THE OBJECT

The object begins vibrating.

Gopal freezes.

### PANEL 1
**TIK.**

### PANEL 2
**TIK.**

### PANEL 3
It rotates.

Slowly.

It points toward a wall.

### PANEL 4
Gopal stands.

The wall is completely ordinary.

He approaches.

### PANEL 5
A tiny mark is scratched into the wood.

The same symbol.

But this one is much older.

## PAGE 19 — SOMEONE IS ALREADY THERE

Gopal touches the symbol.

A voice speaks behind him.

**VOICE:**  
“You shouldn't touch that.”

Gopal turns.

### PANEL 2
A boy about Gopal's age stands in the alley.

He has a small pack over one shoulder.

His expression is completely serious.

### PANEL 3
Gopal points at the symbol.

**GOPAL:**  
“You know what this is?”

The boy looks at the navigation object.

### PANEL 4
**BOY:**  
“I know what it means.”

Gopal's eyes widen.

### PANEL 5
**GOPAL:**  
“What?”

The boy answers:

**BOY:**  
“It means someone is looking for the road again.”

## PAGE 20 — FINAL PAGE

Gopal looks at him.

### PANEL 1
**GOPAL:**  
“Who?”

The boy doesn't answer.

### PANEL 2
Instead, he looks toward the dark horizon.

### PANEL 3
Far beyond Orrin, somewhere in the forest...

A narrow line of pale light appears between the trees.

### PANEL 4
The boy whispers:

**BOY:**  
“Not who.”

### PANEL 5 — LARGE FINAL PANEL

Gopal stares toward the distant light.

**BOY:**  
“How many.”

### FINAL CAPTION

**Some roads disappear because nobody remembers them.**

**Others disappear because somebody doesn't want them found.**

**And sometimes...**

**a road can disappear because it is still moving.**

# END OF CHAPTER 3
