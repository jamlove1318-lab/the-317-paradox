# CHAPTER 2 — THE RIVER DOESN'T CARE

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — opening chapter rewrite V3  
**Saga:** The First Horizon  
**Arc:** Leaving Veyra

## Chapter Purpose

Chapter 2 continues directly from Gopal's first night away from Veyra.

There is no new major character.

There is no new grand mystery.

The chapter lets the reader actually travel with Gopal.

The river, boat, passengers, weather, food, work and small problems become the adventure.

Gopal discovers that traveling is not the same thing as imagining travel.

His first day away from home becomes funny, uncomfortable, beautiful and slightly dangerous.

The chapter also begins establishing the Aster River Reach as a lived-in region rather than a backdrop.

Sera does **not** become a major character yet. Her introduction is reserved for the next phase of the journey, when her existing life naturally intersects with Gopal's.
