# CHAPTER 1 — THE ROAD BEGINS

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — opening chapter rewrite V3  
**Saga:** The First Horizon  
**Arc:** Leaving Veyra

## Chapter Purpose

The first chapter is not about explaining the world.

It is about making the reader want to follow Gopal.

The chapter establishes:

- Veyra as a real home rather than a disposable starting village.
- Gopal's humor, curiosity, kindness, impulsiveness, and tendency to interfere.
- His family and childhood friendships.
- The ordinary life he is leaving behind.
- The fact that the world already contains countless lives and events unrelated to him.
- His first small mistake: helping before understanding.
- The emotional reason he leaves.
- The first tiny visual thread of the wider mystery, without explaining it.

The chapter must not reveal:

- the ultimate antagonist
- the full power system
- Gopal's hidden power
- the deepest world mystery
- a major villain
- a grand prophecy
- a chosen-one explanation
- the eventual shape of Gopal's dream

The reader should finish knowing two things:

**Gopal loves home.**

**Gopal needs to see what is beyond it.**

Morning mist hangs over a broad river.

Veyra sits beside it: houses, workshops, market stalls, a bridge, small boats, vegetable carts, laundry lines, children running.

It is not spectacular.

It is alive.

**CAPTION:**

> Veyra was not the kind of place people wrote stories about.

**Panel 2.**

A riverboat passes.

**CAPTION:**

> People came here because they were going somewhere else.

**Panel 3.**

A baker opens his shop.

A fisherman argues with a customer.

**FISHERMAN:**
"That fish was bigger yesterday."

**CUSTOMER:**
"Then you should have sold it yesterday."

**Panel 4.**

A dog lies directly in the middle of the road.

A loaded cart approaches.

The driver pulls the reins.

**DRIVER:**
"Move."

The dog does not move.
