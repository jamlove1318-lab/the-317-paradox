# Chapter 06 — Aster Reach

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — STORY DRAFT
**Saga:** 01 — The First Horizon

## 24-page manga script

### Page 01 — Too Many Directions
Gopal stands where three roads leave the dock. He asks which way to go, then admits he doesn't know where he is going. The crewman worries this is exactly how Gopal gets into trouble.

### Page 02 — The Crowd
Gopal enters the dock district and is carried sideways by the crowd. Carts, animals, workers and travelers move around him. He apologizes to three people in succession.

### Page 03 — Food
The smells overwhelm him. Fish, grilled meat, fruit, rice cakes, sweets and unfamiliar spices compete for his attention. His stomach makes the decision.

### Page 04 — The First Bite
He chooses a bright red snack despite a vendor's warning. He takes one bite and refuses to admit the food is destroying him.

### Page 05 — Bran
Bran recognizes him as the boy who jumped into the river. Gopal insists he was “falling voluntarily.” Bran tells him to drink water.

### Page 06 — Recovery
Gopal drinks and declares the food evil. Bran says it is the best stall in the district. Gopal decides Aster Reach may be dangerous.

### Page 07 — Bran's Work
Bran counts cargo and argues over a delivery. Gopal offers to investigate. Both Bran and the porter immediately refuse.

### Page 08 — Money
Gopal asks where he can sleep. Bran asks how much money he has. The answer is embarrassing. Gopal's “question asking” is rejected as a job skill.

### Page 09 — Boarding House
Bran directs him to an inexpensive riverside boarding house where travelers can work for part of their stay. Gopal misunderstands Bran's joke about swimming there.

### Page 10 — Market
On the way, Gopal sees cloth, tools, instruments, books and toys from distant regions. The city is already teaching him through ordinary commerce.

### Page 11 — Wooden Animal
He finds a small wooden animal, negotiates badly, and buys it. It becomes a tiny object he keeps for no grand reason except that he liked it.

### Page 12 — Boarding House Entrance
The cook tells Gopal that if he stays, he works. Gopal asks what the job is. The answer: whatever needs doing.

### Page 13 — First Work
Gopal carries laundry and folds it badly. He carries firewood and drops one piece. The cook says he will learn.

### Page 14 — House Rhythm
The boarding house is introduced as a living micro-community: travelers, workers, musicians, children, deliveries, arguments and meals.

### Page 15 — Small Room
Gopal gets a tiny room overlooking the river. He places the wooden animal on the windowsill and realizes he has a place to sleep.

### Page 16 — Home Message
A message arrives from Mira: Rajan thinks Gopal is eating too much; Nemi thinks he is eating too little. Gopal laughs and writes that everyone is probably wrong, then quietly adds that he misses them.

### Page 17 — Night Market
Gopal explores after work. Lights, music, food stalls, dancing, awkward romance and children playing fill the street.

### Page 18 — Bran Again
Bran exits a shop. Gopal asks whether he lives here. Bran explains that working somewhere and living somewhere are different things. Gopal files away the distinction.

### Page 19 — River Lesson
Bran tells him to watch who comes and goes on the river. Everyone leaves something behind. Gopal likes the sentence; Bran jokingly says good sentences cost money.

### Page 20 — Civic Guards
Civic Guards separate two arguing merchants, help a lost child and inspect a damaged cart. They are neither glorified nor demonized; they are people doing difficult ordinary work.

### Page 21 — Ordinary Authority
Gopal notices that a city needs people whose job is preventing small problems from becoming disasters. This quietly establishes the local law system.

### Page 22 — Balcony
At night Gopal watches the river lights and thinks he could stay here. He smiles because he knows that staying is already becoming emotionally possible.

### Page 23 — Familiarity Begins
He recognizes the evening ferry horn, a bakery cart and the boarding-house footsteps. Aster Reach is no longer just a place he arrived at.

### Page 24 — A Place to Return To
Full spread: Aster Reach at night. Gopal sleeps upstairs, the wooden animal on the sill. Caption: “For the first time since leaving Veyra, Gopal had a place in the world that wasn't home... but wasn't entirely strange anymore.”

## Continuity
Bran is now a recurring supporting character with his own trade life. The boarding house becomes Gopal's temporary base. Civic Guards exist naturally. Gopal has begun emotionally investing in Aster Reach. No Sera yet beyond the groundwork that will lead to her introduction.
