# THE LAST EXPEDITION — FIFTEEN MAJOR INCIDENTS

> Status: PROVISIONAL

The historical spine currently has fifteen incidents: 1) The Impossible Record — the Founder and Navigator establish that the legendary destination may be physically real. 2) The Nine Agreements — nine groups cooperate despite incompatible political aims. 3) The Great Departure — the expedition becomes a moving society, not merely a fleet. 4) The Three-Seas Split — the nine branches take different routes, creating contradictory histories. 5) The Hollow Storm — geography itself behaves abnormally. 6) The First Passage Key — an object is proven to make an otherwise impossible passage temporarily possible; it is not a map or coordinate. 7) The Moving City — families, craftsmen, markets, schools and cultures turn the expedition into a civilization. 8) The Crown's Offer — political control of the Keys becomes the first major fracture. 9) The Second Key — the expedition discovers that the Keys are components of an older access system. 10) The Horizon Gathering — the nine branches and thousands of people converge before the final approach. 11) The Key That Changed Hands — a third Key is secretly moved, creating the historical seed of the lost-Key legends. 12) The Nine Conditions — Keys alone are insufficient; environment, timing, knowledge, cooperation and different human abilities are all required. 13) The Arrival — the expedition reaches the destination and discovers evidence that someone was there before them. 14) The Night the Expedition Ended — a decision involving the access system fractures the expedition; deaths, disappearances and contradictory records follow. 15) The Last Return — years later, nine ships appear in nine places; most aboard are dead, one survivor returns with a Passage Key, confirms the destination is real and reveals that the expedition was not first.

## Structural rule
Every historical incident must change at least one person, the expedition, and the modern world. The expedition should feel like civilization moving through history rather than nine ships moving through scenery.

## Passage Key rule
A Passage Key is an actual physical requirement for the final passage. It does not point to another clue, choose a hero, or solve the journey alone. The eventual access system requires multiple physical components plus conditions, knowledge, timing and human cooperation.

## Open questions
- exact identities and relationships of the historical cast;
- exact number and nature of Passage Keys;
- why the nine ships return simultaneously;
- why most passengers are dead;
- what the predecessor civilization/person was;
- what caused the fracture;
- how the returned Key becomes lost and how its history is deliberately confused;
- what the destination actually is.

These remain PROVISIONAL until the destination is designed backward from this history.
