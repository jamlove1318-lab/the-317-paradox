# Chapter 04 — Five Crates

**Status:** PROVISIONAL — STORY DRAFT
**Saga:** 01 — The First Horizon

Chapter 4 continues directly from Chapter 3. The boat has stopped at a small riverside loading settlement. The missing-crate disagreement continues, but the answer is deliberately ordinary: the merchant changed the destination after the buyer changed the order and forgot to update the manifest.

## 24-page manga structure

1. Morning loading point; boats, workers, carts and river trade establish the settlement.
2. Merchant is still certain one crate is missing; Gopal refuses to let the question go.
3. Five crates are counted again; comedy as Gopal dramatically confirms the obvious.
4. Gopal questions a dock worker who sees hundreds of crates every day.
5. The merchant examines painted cargo marks; Gopal notices an older scratched mark.
6. The mark triggers the merchant's memory of changing the destination.
7. Gopal and the merchant search the dock office records.
8. The record shows one crate was redirected to Eastbank.
9. The merchant remembers the buyer changed the order yesterday.
10. Gopal realizes the crate was never stolen or mysteriously moved.
11. Bran is glimpsed arguing over his own cargo; the river's trade network gains another recurring face.
12. Bran recognizes Gopal as the boy who jumped into the river; their banter begins.
13. Gopal watches ordinary people argue over ordinary things because everyone wants something.
14. The merchant thanks Gopal for caring, while admitting Gopal made everyone tired.
15. Gopal records: “Sometimes the answer is boring.”
16. The crew loads the remaining cargo; Gopal waits to be asked before helping.
17. Bran warns Gopal that the north market will consume his money.
18. A quiet conversation reveals some river routes are avoided because they cause more trouble than they are worth; no villain is named.
19. The boat leaves the loading settlement and Bran's boat goes another direction.
20. Gopal receives a message from Veyra asking whether he is eating.
21. He realizes he misses home without becoming unhappy about leaving it.
22. The river becomes faster and the crew prepares for a difficult stretch.
23. Gopal asks how they know where to go; the crew explains that maps help, but the river gets the final word.
24. The river bends toward distant structures. Aster Reach is ahead.

## Character beat
Gopal has not “solved a mystery” in the heroic sense. He has learned that curiosity can uncover something real even when the answer is simply human forgetfulness. He also begins learning that other people can carry responsibility for their own problems.

## Continuity

- Bran remains a recurring supporting character, not a crew member.
- Aster Reach is now visibly close.
- No Rask reveal.

- No hidden-power reveal.

**Ending feeling:** The river is becoming a road with history, work and people, and Gopal is about to enter a much larger lived-in place.
